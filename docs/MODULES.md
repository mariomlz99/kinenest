Linux teaches files, directories, editing and shell basics. ROS covers the environment, terminal topics, packages, pub/sub, messages/parameters, services/launch and actions.

Each module keeps separate saved work. TF & Odometry opens directly from the third homepage card into its own learning view and mini-simulator; prior TF sessions are retained. Its five exercises appear only there. The ROS Basics Playground does not contain TF or odometry lessons. The simulated ROS runtime still supports their topics and interfaces.

TF lookup/point exercises have runtime checks; the module does not award course completion badges and its homepage counter is `0/0 completed`. Linux and ROS Basics retain their seven-unit progress. ROS Playground and Free Lab also have independent workspaces. Free Lab has no checker. Reset affects the selected workspace or unit.

Homepage progress is local to this browser. It is not an account-synchronized certificate. Coming-soon cards do not open lessons.
