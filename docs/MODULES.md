Linux teaches files, directories, editing and shell basics. ROS covers the environment, terminal topics, packages, pub/sub, messages/parameters, services/launch and actions.

Each module keeps separate saved work. TF & Odometry opens directly from the third homepage card into its existing isolated TF workspace; prior TF sessions are retained. Its lookup/point exercises have runtime checks; odometry is exploratory and does not award a completion badge. Linux and ROS Basics retain their seven-unit progress. ROS Playground and Free Lab also have independent workspaces. Free Lab has no checker. Reset affects the selected workspace or unit.

Homepage progress is local to this browser. It is not an account-synchronized certificate. Coming-soon cards do not open lessons.
